# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility
Employees may request work-from-home arrangements when their role and business requirements permit remote work.

## Approval
Regular work-from-home arrangements require manager approval and must follow the employee's team scheduling requirements.

## Availability
Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during their assigned working hours and attend required meetings.

## Security
Company information accessed remotely must use approved devices, authentication mechanisms, and secure network practices.

## Exceptions
Business-critical events or customer requirements may require employees to work from an approved office location.
