# Employee Benefits Guide

## Health Coverage
Eligible employees may participate in the company's approved health insurance program subject to the terms of the current benefits plan.

## Learning
Employees may request reimbursement or support for approved professional learning activities according to the learning budget rules.

## Wellness
The employee wellness program provides access to approved wellness resources and activities.

## Benefit Changes
Benefits may change between plan years. Employees should consult the current benefits enrollment information for applicable terms.

## Eligibility
Specific benefits can depend on employment status, plan rules, and eligibility requirements.
