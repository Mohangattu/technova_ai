# Employee Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Eligible full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

## Sick Leave
Employees may take sick leave when they are unable to work due to illness. Supporting documentation may be requested according to HR procedures.

## Approval
Planned leave should be submitted through the employee portal and approved by the employee's designated manager.

## Carry Forward
Unused annual leave may be carried forward according to the current annual HR rules.

## Emergency Leave
Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible when an emergency prevents advance leave submission.
